description: File one reviewer's verdict on a roadmap leaf as a review record
---

Thin stub — validates review notes and files them as `work/reviews/<NODE_ID>/REVIEW-<model>.md`. Generated by specyrd {{SPECYRD_VERSION}}.

Read only `work/reviews/<NODE_ID>/PACKET.md` (from `/specyrd-review-packet`). Copy `work/review-record.template.md`, fill in its front matter with one `acceptance` entry per `A#` in the packet's brief, and set `commit` to the packet's **Commit under review** (quoted).

Then, from the **repository root**:

```bash
specy-road review record <NODE_ID> --model <id> --verdict approve --file <notes.md>
```

- `approve` may not list failing acceptance or `required_changes`; use `block`.
- The record counts only for the commit it names. Any later commit — a merge included — needs a new review.
- `--model` must be a roster id in `roadmap/orchestration.yaml` on the integration branch; the family comes from the roster. `human` is refused here: a human approves interactively in `mark-implementation-reviewed`.

Do **not** run `mark-implementation-reviewed` yourself; the coordinator does. See `docs/orchestration.md`.
