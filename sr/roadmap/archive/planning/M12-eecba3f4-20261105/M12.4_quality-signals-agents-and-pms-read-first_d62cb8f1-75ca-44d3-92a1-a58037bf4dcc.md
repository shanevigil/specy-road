## Intent

Give agents and PMs one place to read, before planning or routing work, where a project is weak: which areas keep needing extra review rounds or tripping integrity checks, and which known shortcuts are still open. Both signals land in `roadmap-context.md` (the digest agents already load first), so no new file has to be discovered.

## Approach

- M12.4.1 computes per-area quality from review history and adds a "Quality by area" digest section, plus optional routing of extra review to weak areas.
- M12.4.2 adds a tracked debt ledger (`roadmap/debt.yaml`) with commands to manage it and to turn an entry into a leaf; open debt is listed in the digest. It depends on M12.4.1 so both digest sections are designed together (ordering, size caps, determinism).
- Shared constraints: digest output must be deterministic and identical on every checkout (no wall-clock input; only tracked or reproducible data), each section is bounded in length, and an empty source prints nothing.
- Done when both leaves are Complete and `specy-road digest --check` passes on this repo and the dogfood fixture.

## Tasks / checklist

- [ ] M12.4.1 — per-area quality section and opt-in `quality_routing`.
- [ ] M12.4.3 — per-model review evidence (`models stats`) and opt-in evidence scoring in `orchestrate plan`.
- [ ] M12.4.2 — debt ledger, `debt add|list|close|plan`, digest listing, validate warning.
- Out of scope: charts or GUI views (PM Gantt), cross-project aggregation, scoring individual models or people.
