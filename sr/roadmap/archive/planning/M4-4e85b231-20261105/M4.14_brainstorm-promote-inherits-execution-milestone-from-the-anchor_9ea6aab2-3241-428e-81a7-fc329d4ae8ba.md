## Intent

brainstorm promote inherits execution_milestone from the anchor. Done when:
- Promoted leaves carry the anchor's execution_milestone unless --set execution_milestone overrides it
- A Human-led leaf is the only one that needs --set; tests cover inherit and override

## Approach

Copy the session or `--under` parent's `execution_milestone` onto each promoted leaf after that parent is confirmed to exist; do not invent a missing-anchor default. Root promotion and a parent with no (or non-enum) `execution_milestone` leave the field off. `--set execution_milestone=` is the only override — Human-led under an Agentic-led parent is the case that needs it. Verify inherit, omit-when-unset, override, unknown-anchor, and root promotion with `pytest tests/test_brainstorm_promote.py`.
