## Intent

A decision made during a brainstorm is recorded as a decision, not as a parked or rejected idea, and the frame can change with history.

## Approach

Decided is an idea status; the sheet section is rendered by promote for the anchor. Frame history is a list on the session; prompts render the current answers plus a provisional marker.

## Tasks / checklist

- [ ] policy kind + decide
- [ ] promote section
- [ ] frame amend/provisional/confirm
- [ ] Tests
