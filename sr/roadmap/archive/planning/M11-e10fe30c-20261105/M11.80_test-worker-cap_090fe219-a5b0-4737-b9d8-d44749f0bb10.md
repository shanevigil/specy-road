## Intent

Proposed from field practice reported in a 2026-11 engineering newsletter essay and from the 0.3.1 coordination data (2026-11-03). With several executors and reviewers each running the test suite with one worker per core, load reached about 31 on an 18-core machine, the landing suite took about 15 minutes instead of about 5, and one reviewer's tests overran the harness's background-task limit and were killed.

## Approach

Read the key in git_workflow_config next to land_check_cmd; render one sentence in the executor brief and the reviewer prompt. Verify with the touched test files and file-limits. If a file you need is outside the touch zones, add it on your branch with specy-road edit-node M11.80 --add touch_zones=<path>, commit, and say why in the summary instead of stopping.
