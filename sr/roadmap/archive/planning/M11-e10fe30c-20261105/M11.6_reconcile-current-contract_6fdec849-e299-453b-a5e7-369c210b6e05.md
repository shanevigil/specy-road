## Intent

Found in rc2 field use (2026-11-02) with an overnight coordinator running worktree and clone lanes. After the coordinator merged the integration branch into a leaf branch and widened its zones, reconcile still flagged the newly allowed paths and the merged-in files as outside the touch zones, and reported the brief changed whenever main moved; every second-round leaf tripped it.

## Approach

Read zones from the claim row at reconcile time, compute changed paths against the merge base so merged-in files drop out, and hash only the leaf's own contract for the brief-changed check. Verify with the touched test files and file-limits.
