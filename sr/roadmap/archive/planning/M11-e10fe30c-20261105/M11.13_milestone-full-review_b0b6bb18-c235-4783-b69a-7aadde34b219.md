## Intent

Maintainer decision (2026-11-02): with delta reviews on for small fix rounds, a milestone gets one full review of its combined change before it rolls up to Complete, so cross-leaf regressions that leaf-sized reviews miss are caught once per milestone.

## Approach

Hold the ancestor rollup while the milestone's full review is pending; build the milestone packet from the merge base of its earliest leaf to the integration tip; reuse the leaf gate for the two-family approval. Verify with the rollup, packet and gate tests and file-limits.
