## Intent

Found in rc field use (2026-11-02). In long orchestration chats, questions needing a human decision were buried among worker, review and CI status and scrolled away; several were asked twice, some were already answered in a sheet's decision block, and many lacked what was needed to answer (what the item is, why now, what each option does, the recommendation, the deadline). Human-only actions were mixed with agent work. This was the reporting maintainer's highest-impact item. A tracked ledger also lets a new session take over open decisions.

## Approach

Keep the ledger a small YAML list beside registry.yaml; reuse the schema validation path; the skill text change is the larger part of the value. Verify with the touched test files and file-limits.
