## Intent

A promoted leaf could not be picked up: pickup skips a leaf whose ## Approach is still placeholder text (M3.1), while the adopter's promoted sheets led them to expect the Approach would be written at pickup, so the leaf waited forever. `--plan` reported the skip correctly; the toolkit's own M12 phase hit the same skip. The fix keeps the guard and makes the next step obvious and cheap to delegate.

## Approach

- `planning_sheet_bootstrap.PROMOTE_PLACEHOLDER` text (and the scaffold check keyed on it) says pickup skips the leaf until ## Approach is written and names `specy-road author-approach <ID>`; keep detection working for sheets with the old placeholder.
- New `specy_road/bundled_scripts/author_approach.py` and a script-map line in `cli.py`; usage in `cli_usage.py`.
- orchestrate plan and grind-session --plan: one "needs Approach" line using `planning_scaffold_check.pickup_unauthored_leaves`.
- `brainstorm_promote.py` and `docs/brainstorming.md` are at or near their caps: reword only.
