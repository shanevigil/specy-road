## Intent

An executor wrote `ready --run "bash -lc \"... \$f ...\""`; the outer shell expanded `$f` to empty, the loop ran `wc -l ""`, the tail showed `/bin/sh: cannot open ""`, and READY recorded `exit: 0`. Reviewers read that as passing evidence. The adopter's metrics name one executor that twice produced misleading evidence. `ready` should flag an output tail that shows a failed command, and offer a form that quoting cannot break.

## Approach

- New `specy_road/ready_run_checks.py`: `run_check(cmd)` moved from `review_gate_ready.py`, `run_argv(argv)` (no shell), `FAILURE_MARKERS` (the four strings), and `tail_warning(tail) -> str | None` returning the first matching line.
- `review_gate_ready.cmd_ready`: `--run-argv` parsed with `json.loads`, validated before any run; argv runs share the `--run` loop and refusal.
- `review_packet._ready_verify`: one line printing `**Warning:** <line>` when the row has `warning`; `review_packet.py` is at 392, so at most two lines.
- READY rows are not schema-validated per key (`review_files.load_ready`), so `warning` needs no schema edit; check that `ready_semantic_errors` accepts it.
