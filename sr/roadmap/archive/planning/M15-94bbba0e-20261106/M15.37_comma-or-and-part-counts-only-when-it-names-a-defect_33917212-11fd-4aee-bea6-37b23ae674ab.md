## Intent

Approving reviewers write lists and compound sentences. `review record` refuses them as "two observations", even when nothing in them names a defect. Real refusals (rc4, 2026-11-05):

- "No internal imports, mock assertions or defensive branches were added; not a defect because ..." was refused as two observations joined by ','.
- An Opus reviewer's "... and checks both the pass case and the fail case, which is not a defect because it is the exact scenario A2 describes." was refused as two observations joined by 'and'.

The rule M11.75 added (837a05bf) only needs to stop one explanation from covering a second, named defect. This leaf keeps that check and stops refusing harmless lists. It is the top rc4 priority because the rule decides which approvals can be filed (maintainer 2026-11-05).

## Approach

- Root cause: `_PARTS` splits on every comma and on ' and ', and `_second_observation` counts any part of three words or more that does not open with a `_CONTINUES` word as a separate observation.
- Change only `_second_observation`. A part joined by ',' or ' and ' counts only when `_named_defect` finds a defect word in it. When one does, the text before it counts from two words, so "X fails and Y crashes" and "X fails, Y crashes" are still refused (today they pass, because "X fails" has fewer than three words). Parts joined by ';', a sentence end, or ' but ' / ' while ' and the other conjunctions are counted as they are today.
- A prototype of the rule passes 99 of the 100 existing tests. The only failure is the 'cache miss is expected and the cache key ignores the branch name' row, which the new rule accepts by design; update that row to expect None. All four quoted examples give the expected result.
- Keep the M11.75 commit history (8418e906, 837a05bf) and the M15.17 quoting of refused lines (d5f88a7b) as they are.
