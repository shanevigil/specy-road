## Intent

A harness parked on a prompt or a hung tool burns wall-clock with no signal; the wrapper sees every output line, so silence is cheap to detect. A stalled mark on the record and one event per silence period let the coordinator act on the next turn instead of discovering it an hour later.

## Approach

In `specy_road/worker_runner.py` `run()` update `last_output_at` on the record at most every five seconds (`mutate` under the lock is not free). Add the field to `worker-record.schema.json` and `stalled` to the event schema. In `specy_road/worker_ops.py` the sync and list paths compare against `policy.stall_after_seconds` (default in `orchestration_config.py`), append the event once per silence and clear on new output. Render the mark in `worker_cli_render.py`. Docs: flags table and event list in `docs/orchestration-workers.md` (374 lines, cap 400), policy table in `docs/orchestration.md`. Verify with `pytest tests/test_worker_faults.py tests/test_worker_state.py`.

Converge verdict (brainstorm `agentic-performance`): Principle 9: one observable behaviour, one test. Docs: orchestration-workers.md states and flags table.
