## Intent

Found while coordinating M10.12 (2026-11-02). M10.7 capped the inlined diff by lines, but a rebuilt minified GUI bundle is a handful of very long lines: the M10.12 packet came out at about 535 KB in under 1,500 lines, and the first reviewer filed no record. Generated artefacts are also not worth a reviewer's attention line by line.

## Approach

In `specy_road/bundled_scripts/review_packet.py`, keep the per-file whole-or-listed approach from M10.7 and add a byte budget alongside the line budget, with a `--max-diff-bytes` option. Treat a small default set of generated paths (the packaged GUI bundle, plus `.gitattributes` `linguist-generated` entries) as always listed, never inlined. If the module nears its cap, move the budget logic into a helper module. Verify with `tests/test_review_packet.py`, file-limits and the docs line.
