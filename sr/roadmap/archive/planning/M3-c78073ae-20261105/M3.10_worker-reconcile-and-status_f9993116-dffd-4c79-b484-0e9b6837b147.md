## Intent

A delegated worker can hand off from a linked worktree, reconcile reports only real deviations, and finish says what it will push before pushing.

## Approach

Each item is a reporting or boundary fix at an existing seam; none changes what the gate requires. Reconcile's zone test should reuse the packet's boundary matching so the two agree.
