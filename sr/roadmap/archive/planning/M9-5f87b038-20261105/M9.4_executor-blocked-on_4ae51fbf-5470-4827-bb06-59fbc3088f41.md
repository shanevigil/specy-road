## Intent

Agents that cannot meet a criterion tend to edit tests; a channel to stop and flag it measurably reduces that (ImpossibleBench, Gomez 2026). Give the executor that channel in READY and keep the gate unmet while it is used. Design: `docs/design-notes/regression-prevention.md`.

## Approach

Add `blocked_on` to `specy_road/schemas/handoff.schema.json` and the READY template; check acceptance ids where READY files are loaded (`review_files.py`). Add one rule to the executor steps in `do_next_prompt._finish_instruction_lines`. In `review_gate.evaluate_gate`, a current READY with `blocked_on` adds a problem; `review_cli` maps it to state `executor_blocked` (exit 1, like `blocked`). `review_cli.py` is at 369 lines: keep additions small. Verify with `pytest tests/test_review_evidence.py tests/test_review_files.py` and the new tests.
