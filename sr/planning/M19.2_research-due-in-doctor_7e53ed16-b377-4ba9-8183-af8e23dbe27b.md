## Intent

`validate` should answer one question: is the roadmap correct? Research-register reminders (an entry past its cadence or never run) are housekeeping, not correctness, and today they print on every validate run. Move them to `doctor`, the command that already reports what in a checkout needs attention, so validate prints nothing about research.

## Approach

- Home: `doctor`, not a new `research due` subcommand. doctor is already the "what needs attention here" report, M16.4.1 makes it the one cleanup path, and there is no `research` command group to hang a subcommand on — adding one would grow the command surface the M16 rules hold flat.
- Move `warn_research_register` (and `_register_entry_problems`) out of specy_road/bundled_scripts/validate_roadmap.py into the doctor code; the problems found and their wording stay the same, so it is a move, not a rewrite.
- Depends on: M16.4.1 (doctor consolidation), and the phase's dependency on the 0.3.1 tag gate M16.8.9.
