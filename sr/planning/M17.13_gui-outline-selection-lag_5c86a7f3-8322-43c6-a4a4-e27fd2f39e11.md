## Intent

M17.1 question 10 (maintainer, 2026-11-06: fix it in 0.3.1). The rc4 final sandbox (2026-11-06, F1) measured the outline selection updating about 225 ms after a row click (224-233 ms on 5 rows). A toolbar action clicked inside that window acts on the previously selected row with no cue: 2 of 2 immediate "Add task below" clicks created the node under the wrong row; 5 of 5 were right after 800 ms.

Cause: `OutlineTable.tsx`'s row click handler selects the row in a 220 ms `setTimeout`, so a double click can open the edit modal instead.

Out of scope: other outline interactions; keyboard navigation.

## Approach

- Select on the click itself. A double click still opens the edit modal; the first click of it has already selected the same row, so nothing else changes.
- Title-click editing, long press and dependency-edit clicks keep their current behaviour.
- The click / double-click decision moves to a pure helper (`outlineRowClick.ts`) so vitest can check it with fake timers.

## Tasks / checklist

- [ ] outlineRowClick.ts helper and tests.
- [ ] OutlineTable.tsx uses it; toolbar actions read the selection at click time.
- [ ] npm ci, lint, test, build; static rebuilt; CHANGELOG.

## Tier

behavioural: the bug is timing in the real UI, and a pure-helper test can pass while the component or the toolbar still acts on the old selection.

## Done but wrong

- The toolbar still acts on the previous row. Check: in the built GUI, a row click followed at once by "Add task below" creates the node under the clicked row in 5 of 5 tries, the case the rc4 sandbox measured at 2 of 2 wrong.
- A double click on an unselected row no longer opens the edit modal, because the first click's selection re-renders the row. Check: in the built GUI, a double click on an unselected row opens the modal for that row.

## If this fails

The assumption most likely to break is that the toolbar handlers read the selection current at click time once the timer is gone. If a handler keeps a stale selection from an earlier render, the coordinator decides whether that fix stays in this leaf (`App.tsx` and `usePmGuiHandlers.ts` are zoned) or becomes its own.

## Coordinator resolution (2026-11-07)

The handler is `handleTitleClick`, not `handleRowClick`.
