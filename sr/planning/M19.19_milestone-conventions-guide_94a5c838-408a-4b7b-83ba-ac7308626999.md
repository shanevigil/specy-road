## Intent

The maintainer's 2026-11-05 planning rules (themed milestones; new work never goes into an old milestone; one fixes milestone per release, rc4's fixes in M21 and rc5's in M22) live only in this repo's own practice. Adopters get no hint and end up with a catch-all milestone that never closes. This leaf writes both rules down as recommendations where adopters and their agents read about roadmap structure.

Out of scope: any validator, notice or refusal (recommend, not enforce); the specyrd managed rules block (agents-rules.md has a line budget pinned by tests/test_lean_rules.py); the dogfood fixture's and sr/'s planning/README.md; making upgrade refresh an adopter's existing planning/README.md.

## Approach

1. docs/roadmap-authoring.md: a short "Milestone conventions" section near "Roadmap layers" (keep the file under its file-limits cap; it is about 400 lines).
2. specy_road/templates/project/planning/README.md: one or two lines with the two rules and a link to the section.
3. suggested_prompts/bootstrap-roadmap.md Phase 2 and suggested_prompts/brainstorm-roadmap.md (the `--under [ANCHOR]` choice): the same two rules, same wording (CLAUDE.md adoption-prompt rule).
4. A small test pinning the shared phrases in the four files, in the style of tests/test_shared_checkout_guidance.py.
