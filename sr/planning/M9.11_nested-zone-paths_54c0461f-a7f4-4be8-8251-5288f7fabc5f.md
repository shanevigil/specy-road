## Intent

Found while coordinating M9.6 (2026-11-02). In this checkout the project root is `sr/` under the git root. M9.6 declared the zone `sr/schemas/` (git-root-relative, like its other zones such as `specy_road/...`), but the review packet listed its changed files as `schemas/git-workflow.schema.json` and so on (project-relative) and flagged all three as outside the touch zones, although the leaf changed exactly what its zone named. The coordinator had to add a project-relative `schemas/` zone to get the leaf reviewed. Any adopter with a nested project root hits the same mismatch.

## Approach

Find where the packet's file list and the zone match are computed (`review_target.touch_zones` and the packet's outside-zone marking, plus `finish_implementation_check`), and normalise both sides to one form, git-root-relative being the natural one since diffs come from git. A zone that is project-relative should still match after normalisation. Add a nested-layout test through the public packet and finish-check paths. Once this lands, M9.6's extra `schemas/` zone can be dropped in a later edit.
