## Intent

An adopter's human made a batch of program decisions, listed open items with options, a recommendation and a needed-by date, and stated a standing reviewer-routing rule. The coordinator recorded all of it in prose. The decisions ledger (`roadmap/decisions.yaml`, `specy-road decisions`) was the right home, but nothing an agent reads at start-up pointed there. The managed `.specyrd/AGENTS.md` block does not mention it. The digest (`roadmap-context.md`) and the leaf status command do not show open entries. `decisions add --kind decision` failed three times before the agent found the flags that kind requires. This leaf makes the ledger discoverable from the places an agent already reads.

**Out of scope:**
- The executor and reviewer prompts: executors and reviewers do not take decisions from the human.
- Id prefixes (M19.15) and the scope-boundary kind (M19.18).
- Applying preferences in `orchestrate plan` (M18.19).
- Renaming or moving the decisions code, which M16.13 and M16.49 do.

## Approach

- Source: adopter feedback on 0.3.1rc3 (item 23, sanitized), filed 2026-11-05.
- The coordinator role prompt (`coordinator_decision_lines`) and `specyrd-orchestrate.md` already cover asking questions and recording preferences. They do not say to record a decision the human makes unprompted. Add one line to each. The managed AGENTS block gets the general rule, because a non-coordinator session (a planner or a plain chat) is where these decisions are usually spoken.
- Digest: read only the tracked `roadmap/decisions.yaml`, never the gitignored `decisions.local.yaml`, so `digest --check` stays deterministic across machines. Keep the new section separate from the existing `## Decisions` section, which renders node `decision` fields.
- Status: show the entries on `review status` (the successor to `handoff status` from M16.13), not on the removed name.
- `--help`: derive the kind-specific required flags from the schema's `allOf` conditionals, so the help and the error cannot drift from the schema.
- Depends on M16.13 (renames `handoff status` to `review status` and reshapes `decisions_cli.py`), M16.49 (moves the decisions code under `specy_road/orchestration/records/`), M16.30 (rewrites `agents-rules.md`) and M16.17 (moves the role-prompt library). Touch zones are the post-M16 paths. M19.7 also edits `agents-rules.md` (agent-file notices). The two are independent, but whichever lands second rebases.
