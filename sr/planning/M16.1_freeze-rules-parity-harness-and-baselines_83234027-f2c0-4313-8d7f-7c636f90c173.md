## Intent

Before any code moves, fix what "behaviour preserved" means and record the starting point. This group holds the parity harness and the review-gate decision table, the maintainer's triage, the governance texts that every later leaf is reviewed against (constitution scope, agent entry and load order), the review-policy evidence that M16.5.12 needs, the baseline metrics, coverage floor and quality baselines, and the two public-scenario test files that must exist before the folds they protect. It also gathers the early human waits: triage, and the policy decisions from the evidence.

## Approach

Leaves run in id order, which is chain order: every leaf's prerequisites have a smaller id, and no edge points to a later group. There are no group-level dependencies; the leaf edges give the order.

Why together: None of these leaves edits production structure. All are measurement, rules or safety nets, and every code leaf depends on at least one of them (M16.1.7 and M16.1.8 directly; M16.1.9 and M16.1.10 through M16.3.3 and M16.3.7). M16.1.4 and M16.1.5 have no code dependencies; placing them here rather than in a late docs group keeps M16.4.1's dependency on M16.1.5 pointing backward.
