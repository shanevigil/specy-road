## Intent

Promote's touch zones can miss what acceptance needs (a response schema directory, an API contract, a mounting page), and a worker obeying "stop on an out-of-zone path" stalls; this toolkit's own executors needed zone additions for seven or more leaves. The brief's zone section ballooned to 8.4 KB on a broad zone by listing every importer.

## Approach

- New `specy_road/zone_coverage.py`: extract path-like tokens from acceptance lines (backticked or slash-containing tokens that exist in the repo or end in a known extension), check them against zones; `orchestrate_plan.py` (372) and grind-session --plan call it once.
- `brief_call_sites.py`: cap per zone at 20 with a count.
