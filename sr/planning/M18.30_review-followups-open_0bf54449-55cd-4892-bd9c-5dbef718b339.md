## Intent

A deferred finding is still a true defect. If its follow-up leaf is never worked, it comes back as an escape: release-level review found 12 of the rc5 grind's 17 escapes. This leaf gives one read-only command that lists open follow-ups from deferrals and fails while any is open or any deferral record is unreadable, so a release check can refuse. It must land before the coordinator form of `review defer` (M18.29). This is the tracking half of R3 in `sr/work/planner-2026-11-11-review-rules/proposal-v2.md`.

**Out of scope:** creating follow-ups (`review defer`, M18.29); blocking `finish` or the gate; a CI job (the runbook step is the refusal until one exists); follow-ups recorded anywhere other than the decisions ledger.

## Approach

- New `specy_road/review_followups.py`. It reads `roadmap/decisions.yaml` through the existing decisions loader and the roadmap index. It picks closed decisions whose answer `review defer` wrote (the `Deferred by` wording that `deferral_in_effect` already checks) and takes the follow-up ids the answer names.
- A malformed answer is reported, never skipped: a skipped record would read as "nothing open".
- A subcommand in `review_cli.py`. It writes nothing.

## Tier

trust boundary, until the operator confirms otherwise: the command only reads, but its exit code becomes a release check in `docs/release-runbook.md`, and its files are on the `sr/tools/tier.py` TRUST list. The planner does not write a "Path floor reviewed" line for it.
