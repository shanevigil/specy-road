## Intent

A sandboxed worker must be able to write refs for its own commits, so it could also move the local integration branch (spike probe 2). This small check closes that. It replaces M22.30, whose other classes the sandbox and M22.31 cover.

## Approach

Compare the local and remote-tracking integration refs at turn start and end in `worker_turn_check.py`. A move is a finding. Coordinator-side only.

## Tasks / checklist

- [ ] Snapshot and compare
- [ ] Tests, CHANGELOG

## Tier

behavioural: a turn could look clean while a ref moved.

## Done but wrong

- A real move goes unreported. Check: a turn that moves the integration ref through the public runner produces the finding.

## If this fails

Another leaf landing during the turn moves the ref legitimately. If that gives false findings, compare against the reflog or a landing record; the coordinator decides.
