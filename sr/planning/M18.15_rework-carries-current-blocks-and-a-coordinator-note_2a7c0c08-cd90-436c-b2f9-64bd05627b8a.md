## Intent

`worker delegate --rework` printed "carries 2 blocking review(s)", including one at an earlier commit that a later commit had fully resolved, while `review list` already marks such records "stale: not the commit under review". The executor reworked an addressed finding. Coordinators also want to give a takeover executor a directive above the reviews, without editing the prompt by hand. M15.6 (landed) made the rework carry the accepted RCA; this applies the same staleness rule and adds a coordinator note.

## Approach

- `role_prompt_rework.py` (135): split `blocking_reviews` into tip and earlier with `same_commit(root, commit, t.commit)`, as `_review_lines` already decides "the branch tip" vs "an earlier commit"; render earlier ones under their own heading; update `rework_check`'s stderr note. `rework_check` needs the branch tip: resolve it with `review_target.resolve_target`.
- Note: `rework_check` writes `work/reviews/<NODE_ID>/REWORK-NOTE.md` with the tip sha on its first line; `rework_lines` reads it and carries it only while the tip matches.
- `worker_cli.py` is at 397 lines: move `cmd_delegate` and the delegate parser to new `bundled_scripts/worker_cli_delegate.py`, importing `_with_op`; add `--note` there. `_with_op`'s default inputs include `note`; bind the file's text.
- Depends on M15.13 (claimed), which owns `worker_cli.py` and `role_prompt_rework.py` now.
