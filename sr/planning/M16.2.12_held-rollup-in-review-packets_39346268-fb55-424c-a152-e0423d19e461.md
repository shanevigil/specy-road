## Intent

Finding F1 of M16.2.7's post-landing review. M16.2.7 pointed `review_target.py` at `roadmap_graph.load_roadmap`, the loader without the milestone full-review hold, and `resolve_target` uses it whenever a caller passes no `nodes=`. The briefs built from `ReviewTarget.nodes` therefore print a held milestone's rollup as Complete. A run confirmed it: on the held-M2 fixture, `roadmap_load.load_roadmap` gives M2 In Progress, while `review_packet.build_packet(root, "M2", ref=None, verify_cmds=[])` prints `- **Status (rollup):** Complete`. A milestone's own full-review packet tells its reviewers it is already done.

Gate and closure decisions are not affected: they read authored status and commits, and the hold check itself passes `nodes=`.

## Tier
