## Intent

Answer "where is this leaf" without a terminal: its worktrees and whether they are dirty or behind, what was handed off for review, what reviewers said and whether their verdicts still apply to HEAD, and what holds it.

Out of scope: diffs, review packets, opening files in a worktree, any action on a review.

## Approach

- One component reused in two places (drawer and edit modal) so the rules live once in the view model.
- "Fresh for HEAD" and the gate summary come from the snapshot (schema 2 via M17.6); the GUI does not recompute gate logic.
- Can run in parallel with M16. Depends on: M17.3.
- M17.1 sign-off (2026-11-06): the edit-modal section stays (question 6); holds show source, id and reason (question 4); one copy button for handoff status (question 2).
