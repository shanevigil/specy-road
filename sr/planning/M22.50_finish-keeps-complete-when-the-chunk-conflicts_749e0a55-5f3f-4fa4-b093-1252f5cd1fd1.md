## Intent

A leaf can land with its Complete status silently lost. Seen 2026-11-08 on M22.43: landing merge 5006d413 (parents 0c27b537 on WIP, ac62b8e8 the leaf's "complete, deregister" commit) resolved `sr/roadmap/phases/M22__b303b5.json` to the integration side. Its message says "finish's Complete status was applied again", but M22.43 stayed Not Started. Coordinator commit 0c27b537 had just changed another node's touch zones in that same chunk. The dispatcher then tried to pick up M22.43 again, and the coordinator restored the status by hand (24c04a5a).

## Approach

Reproduce the case first with a test, then find why `_complete_again` did not take effect: the bookkeeping commit not being found, `_finished_node_key` returning None, or the re-applied edit being lost before staging. Fix that cause. Make the merge message reflect what happened, and stop before pushing when the status cannot be re-applied.

## Tasks / checklist

- [ ] Reproduce with an integration-side edit to the same chunk
- [ ] Fix the cause; stop rather than push when Complete cannot be re-applied
- [ ] Test, CHANGELOG

## Tier

trust boundary: finish and land.

## Done but wrong

- The test passes because the integration side never touched the chunk, so there is no conflict: it asserts the merge took the graph-resolution path (the merge message names the chunk).
- The leaf is Complete but other integration-side edits to the chunk are lost: it asserts the other node's new touch zone survives.

## If this fails

If the cause is outside these zones (for example in the registry resolution), stop and name it; the coordinator decides whether to widen the zones.

## Accepted limits

- Conflicts in files other than roadmap chunks keep today's behaviour.
