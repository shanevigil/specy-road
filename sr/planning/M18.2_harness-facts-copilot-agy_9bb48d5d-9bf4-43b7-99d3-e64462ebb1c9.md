## Intent

M8.6 added candidate profiles for the Copilot CLI (reviewer) and Antigravity `agy` (generic), but not their harness-facts records. These are F23 and F24 in `docs/design-notes/harness-facts-2026-11-02-triage.md`. The M8.6 summary marks them "Not done" because `tests/test_harness_facts.py` requires the fixture ids to equal `worker_state.HARNESSES`, and both files were outside M8.6's zones. Without the records, nothing in `harness-facts.yaml` backs the two profiles' commands.

## Approach

- Source: the 2026-11-05 milestone audit (`sr/work/milestone-audit-2026-11-05.md`, M8 gaps), `sr/work/done/implementation-summary-M8.6.md` ("Not done"), and the triage note (F23, F24).
- Facts only. The two ids do not become worker harnesses: `worker delegate --harness` still accepts the same four values. A launch adapter for either harness would be a separate leaf.
- Take the facts from the profiles' own sources (the vendor pages and changelog that M8.6 cites). Label each fact `documented` with its URL, or `unverified` when no vendor page states it. A `runtime` label needs a capture from an installed binary.
- Depends on M16.17, which moves `harness_facts.py` into `specy_road/orchestration/roster/`. The zones name that end state. The phase starts after the 0.3.1 tag gate.
