## Intent

Source: M22.22 (split 2026-11-06). Two findings from the final sandbox rehearsal (sr/work/sandbox-feedback/2026-11-06-rc4-final-2c45d7e8.md), moved out of M22.22 with their share of its line-change estimate:

1. **F7, upgrade warns about itself.** Before it starts, `specy-road upgrade` prints `warning — AGENTS.md block written by specy-road <old>, running <new>; run \`specy-road upgrade\`.`, telling the person to run the command they are already running.
2. **F8, export's drift line has no fix.** `export --check` reports `drift: <path>` and stops; `digest --check` says `Run: specy-road digest`.

Out of scope: the warning's wording, the `missing <path>` line, and every command other than `upgrade` and `export --check`. `validate` and the rest keep warning.

## Approach

- **F7.** `cli._maybe_warn_running_version_older` returns early for `upgrade`, as it already does for `doctor`. That skips both checks in `version_skew.warn_if_running_version_older` for this one command; upgrade's own report already states the versions it moves between.
- **F8.** In `export_roadmap_md._write_export`'s `--check` branch, the drift line becomes `drift: <path> no longer matches the roadmap. Run: specy-road export`, the digest wording. The gitignore hint after it and exit 1 are unchanged.
