## Intent

Shrink and harden the suite. Committed dedupe worklists are applied per subsystem, public seams replace private patches and wall-clock sleeps, five Hypothesis properties come in, and a CLI smoke lane of at most 50 items keeps every other CLI test in process. The lane is held to the effectiveness measures the maintainer chooses from sr/research/agentic-unit-testing.md; suite time is informational, never a goal or a gate (maintainer, 2026-11-05, decision 5d).

## Approach

Leaves run in id order, which is chain order: every leaf's prerequisites have a smaller id, and no edge points to a later group. There are no group-level dependencies; the leaf edges give the order.

Why together: These are the leaves that remove tests, so `planned_test_changes` (set by the coordinator before pickup) applies to M16.7.2-M16.7.5 and M16.7.7, the leaves the phase sheet's 'Removing tests' rule names: one place for the coordinator's removal bookkeeping. The dedupe leaves compare arc sets on production code as M16.5.14 left it; M16.7.6 then adds only its listed production seams and rewrites tests to them, and a test removal found there is a blocked_on stop (alignment review, 2026-11-05, G6).
