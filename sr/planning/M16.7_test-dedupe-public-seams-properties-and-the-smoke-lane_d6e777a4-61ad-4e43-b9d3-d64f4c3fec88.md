## Intent

Shrink and harden the suite. Committed dedupe worklists are applied per subsystem, public seams replace private patches and wall-clock sleeps, five Hypothesis properties come in, and a CLI smoke lane of at most 50 items halves suite time.

## Approach

Leaves run in id order, which is chain order: every leaf's prerequisites have a smaller id, and no edge points to a later group. There are no group-level dependencies; the leaf edges give the order.

Why together: These are the leaves that remove tests, so `planned_test_changes` (set by the coordinator before pickup) applies to every leaf except M16.7.1 and M16.7.8: one place for the coordinator's removal bookkeeping.
