## Intent

Proposed from field practice reported in a 2026-11 engineering newsletter essay and from the 0.3.1 coordination data (2026-11-03). The coordinator has been choosing a stronger second reviewer by hand for leaves that change the gate, review records or worker launches; a measured reviewer approved commits its paired reviewer blocked about half the time, so which reviewer approves a risky leaf matters, and specy-road cannot express that today.

## Approach

Add the key to the policy schema, evaluate it next to reviewers_distinct_families in review_gate, reuse the zone matching the picker and review packet already use. Verify with the touched test files and file-limits. If a file you need is outside the touch zones, add it on your branch with specy-road edit-node M11.79 --add touch_zones=<path>, commit, and say why in the summary instead of stopping.
