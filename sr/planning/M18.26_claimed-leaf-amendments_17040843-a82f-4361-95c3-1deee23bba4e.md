## Intent

Executors stop when a leaf needs a file outside its touch zones; the coordinator then widens the zones. Make that amendment a first-class edit: it needs a reason, the reason is recorded on the leaf's sheet, the change lands on the leaf branch where the review packet reads zones (M11.49), the stale out-of-zone finding clears, and the review is needed again. Also let a planner change one acceptance line by index instead of resending the whole list.

**Out of scope:** changing zones automatically; amendments to dependencies, title or other fields (they keep today's behaviour); chores (M19.11 adds `chore zones --add`).

## Approach

- Reuse `edit-node`; no new command. The reason requirement applies only to claimed leaves, so planning before pickup is unchanged.
- The sheet's `## Amendments` section keeps the record with the leaf, like the overnight acceptance rewrites the coordinator logged by hand (OD 02:57, 03:05, 03:08).
- Applying on the leaf branch: since M11.49 the packet reads zones from the commit under review, so a zone added only on the integration branch still shows "outside touch zones" (RE Open, `review-packet --ref`). A coordinator zone edit also left the worker's post-run flag set, so the dispatcher kept raising ATTN (RE 2026-11-05 14:53). Clearing covered findings is recorded as a worker event (worker-event schema).
- Zone stops cost a round trip each on about half the pickups (RE 2026-11-03 09:58); this makes the round trip one command.
- Depends on M16.42 (roadmap CRUD fold), M16.53 (tests for the edit-field setters first) and M16.16 (worker modules' end state).
