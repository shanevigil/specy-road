## Intent

D16, Medium (rc4 re-test 2026-11-06). `brainstorm verify` reported 8 of 11 sources `not-found`. Checked by hand, 4 of those 8 quotes are on the page. Two kinds of text difference cause it:

- **Citation markers.** `_norm` in `specy_road/bundled_scripts/brainstorm_verify.py` drops the `<sup>` tag but keeps its text. Wikipedia's "restudying[43] while helping …" therefore never matches the agent's "restudying while helping …", and the same holds for "[44][45][46]".
- **Typographic characters.** `_norm` does not fold typographic apostrophes and quotes. The page has "children’s" and "it’s" (U+2019), while the agent's quote has ASCII `'`. Confirmed: `_norm("…children's education records") in _norm(page)` is False, and True with `’`.

These are the two most common ways an agent's copy of a quote differs from the HTML. Dashes, non-breaking spaces and soft hyphens differ the same way. The promoted sheets then carry "(…, not-found)" next to accurate quotes.

Decision: normalize page text and quote alike, so a match still needs the same words in the same order. Repo sources (`quote_in_file`, a plain-text file at a commit) are unchanged.

Overlaps checked (2026-11-06): no open M21 leaf lists brainstorm_verify.py or docs/brainstorming.md. M22.17 (rc5, the 404 case) edits the same file and depends on this leaf.

## Approach

- In `_norm`, after tags are stripped and entities unescaped:
  - `_CITATION = re.compile(r"\[(?:\d+|[a-z]|note \d+|citation needed)\]", re.I)` is removed.
  - A `str.maketrans` table folds the characters the acceptance names.
  - Zero-width characters (U+200B to U+200D, U+2060, U+FEFF) and U+00AD are deleted.
  - Whitespace is then collapsed and the existing punctuation-space rule runs, so "restudying [43] while" and "restudying[43] while" both become "restudying while".
- A quote made only of a marker normalizes to empty and stays `not-found`, as an empty quote does today.
