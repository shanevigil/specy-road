## Intent

Give known shortcuts a home that planning can act on. Today debt lives in review notes, summaries and chat; nothing lists it, and nothing turns it into work. This leaf adds a small tracked ledger, commands to manage it without hand-editing YAML, a one-step "plan this entry as a leaf", and a short open-debt list in the digest next to M12.4.1's quality section.

## Approach

- **Ledger and schema.** `roadmap/debt.yaml` with `entries: [{id, title, found_in, paths, severity, status, node}]`; `id` like `DEBT-1` (next-id helper modelled on `next_decision_id()` in `specy_road/decisions.py`), `severity` one of `low|medium|high`, `status` `open|planned|closed`, `node` (node_key, shown as display id) required when planned. Schema `debt.schema.json` in `specy_road/templates/project/schemas/`; `refresh-schemas` picks it up automatically because it globs that directory. Read/write/validate helpers follow `read_decisions()` / `write_decisions()` / `schema_errors()` in `specy_road/decisions.py` (add the debt helpers there, or a sibling module if file limits require).
- **Commands.** `specy-road debt add|list|close|plan`, parsed in `specy_road/bundled_scripts/decisions_cli.py` alongside the decisions subcommands, registered as `debt` in `specy_road/cli.py`. `plan <id> --under <PARENT>` adds the leaf through the add-node write (`append_node_to_chunk`, `next_child_id`, as `brainstorm_promote.py` does) with title from the entry, touch zones from `paths`, and one acceptance line naming the entry; it stores the new node's key and sets `planned`.
- **Close on finish.** When finish-this-task completes a leaf, any entry whose `node` is that leaf moves to `closed` in the same commit (hook next to where finish writes the registry/roadmap status in `specy_road/bundled_scripts/finish_task.py`).
- **Digest.** A bounded "Open debt" section in `specy_road/digest.py` (at most 10 lines, high → low severity, then id), placed after M12.4.1's quality section; absent ledger or no open entries → no section. Deterministic: only the tracked ledger is read.
- **Validate.** `specy_road/bundled_scripts/validate_roadmap.py` warns (non-fatal) on an entry whose `node` is missing from the live roadmap and not archived.
- **Docs.** `docs/roadmap-authoring.md` gains a "Debt ledger" section; `init project` does not ship `roadmap/debt.yaml` (the template has no `decisions.yaml` either); `debt add` creates it on first use, and `init project` ships only the schema.
- **Verify.** Tests under `tests/`: schema accept/reject; add → list → plan → finish closes; digest section bounded at 10; validate warning on a dangling node. `specy-road file-limits`.

## Tasks / checklist

- [ ] A1 — `roadmap/debt.yaml` with schema shipped by `init project` and `refresh-schemas`; `specy-road debt add|list|close` manage it (no hand edits needed). Test round-trips each command against the schema.
- [ ] A2 — `specy-road debt plan <id> --under <PARENT>` creates and links a leaf via add-node and marks the entry planned; finishing that leaf with finish-this-task closes the entry. Test covers both.
- [ ] A3 — `roadmap-context.md` lists open debt by severity, at most 10 lines; `validate` warns on an entry whose linked node no longer exists. Tests pin the cap and the warning.
- Out of scope: automatic debt discovery (M12.3 gardening may feed it later), cost/effort estimates, PM GUI views, migrating existing notes into the ledger.
