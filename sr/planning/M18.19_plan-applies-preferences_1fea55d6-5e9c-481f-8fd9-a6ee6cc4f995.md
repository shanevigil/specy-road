## Intent

The human's routing preferences (`kind: preference` in `roadmap/decisions.yaml`) survive context resets. `orchestrate plan` lists them as "not applied" and leaves applying them to the coordinator, who has to remember to. An adopter's human set a standing reviewer rule: a given model reviews every leaf alongside another. The rule was easy to miss, and nothing showed whether a plan honoured it. This leaf has `orchestrate plan`, and `review delegate` when no model is given, apply the preferences they can apply deterministically and say when they do.

**Out of scope:**
- Matching `applies_to`. It is free-text leaf kinds, so a preference that has it stays "not applied" for the coordinator.
- Letting a preference override eligibility, independence, distinct families, strong review or the reviewer count.
- Changing the roster automatically.
- Evidence scoring, which is M18.6.

## Approach

- Source: adopter feedback on 0.3.1rc3 (item 23, the routing part, sanitized), filed 2026-11-05.
- Overlap check: M18.6 changes reviewer scores from review evidence and does not read preferences, so this is a new leaf, not an acceptance line on M18.6. M16.5.10 keeps preference routing byte-for-byte and does not change behaviour.
- Apply a preference after the eligibility filter (M16.5.4 `check_assignment`). A preferred model that survives the filter moves to the front of that role's candidates. Otherwise the preference stays under `preferences_not_applied` with the reason (`applies_to set`, `not rostered`, `unavailable`, `ineligible: <first refusal>`).
- `review delegate` without `--model` calls the same reviewer-selection function as the plan for the resolved target (active or not, so not via `parallel_batches`) and launches the first selected reviewer without a gate-accepted record at the commit under review. With `--model` it behaves as today and adds one notice when an applicable reviewer preference names a different model.
- Update the coordinator text that says the plan never applies preferences.
- Depends on M16.5.3 (orchestrate plan and assign move under `specy_road/orchestration/`), M16.5.4 (one `check_assignment`, used by `review_delegate.py`) and M16.5.10 (decisions code location). Touch zones are the post-M16 paths.
