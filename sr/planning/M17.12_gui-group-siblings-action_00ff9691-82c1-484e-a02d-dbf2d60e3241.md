## Intent

M17.1 question 9 (maintainer, 2026-11-06: yes, in the 0.3.1 GUI). `POST /api/outline/group` exists (the CLI's `group-nodes`), but the outline offers no way to call it (rc4 sandbox GUI test 2026-11-05 part C row 9).

Out of scope: multi-row selection in the outline; changes to the route, its body or group-nodes' rules.

## Approach

- A "Group siblings…" action on a selected row opens a dialog: the row's siblings as checkboxes (the selected row checked), a new parent id (prefilled from the loaded outline with the parent's next unused child number, editable), a title (required), a type (milestone by default, as `GroupOutlineBody`) and "rewrite id references in prose" (off by default, as `--rewrite-refs`).
- Confirm sends one `POST /api/outline/group` with `GroupOutlineBody`'s fields `{into_new, title, node_ids, type, rewrite_refs}` through a new `groupOutline()` in api.ts, wrapped like the other outline mutations (PM write header, pending mutations and the auto-ff retry the move action uses).
- The server's 400 detail is shown verbatim in the dialog; nothing in the outline changes on a refusal.
- The logic lives in a pure helper with vitest rows.
