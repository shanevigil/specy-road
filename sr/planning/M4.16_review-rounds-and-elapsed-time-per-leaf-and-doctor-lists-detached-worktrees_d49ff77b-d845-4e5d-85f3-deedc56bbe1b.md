## Intent

Review rounds and elapsed time per leaf, and doctor lists detached worktrees. Done when:
- handoff status and review list report, per leaf, the number of review rounds (distinct reviewed commits) and the elapsed time from the first review to the latest
- docs/orchestration.md states that two or more review rounds per leaf are normal under a two-family policy
- doctor also lists detached worktrees of the repository (for example ones a review recipe left behind) and, with --fix, removes those with no uncommitted changes

## Approach

Rounds and elapsed time are derived, not stored: from the review files under `work/reviews/<ID>/`, count the distinct `commit:` values across reviewer records (one round per reviewed commit) and take the span from the earliest to the latest review `date`/file time. Compute it once in `specy_road/review_files.py` and print it from both `handoff status` and `review list` in `specy_road/bundled_scripts/review_cli.py` (one line per leaf, `rounds: N, first to latest: <duration>`; `n/a` with a single review). Add the sentence about two or more rounds being normal under a two-family policy to `docs/orchestration.md` (and `docs/pm-llm-review.md` if it describes the gate). For doctor, list `git worktree list --porcelain` entries marked `detached`; with `--fix`, remove only those whose `git status --porcelain` is empty, and report the rest. Tests drive the public CLI: a leaf with reviews at two commits, a single-review leaf, a clean and a dirty detached worktree.
