## Intent

Let one roster entry spread its workers over several accounts of the same harness, keep a resumed session on the account that holds it, and move work to another account when one hits its usage limit. The 0.3.1 grind did this with `split.txt` (weights per Claude config directory), `split-counts.json` and session-account lookup in `dispatch.py`, after fix rounds failed in seconds on a weekly-limited account (RE 2026-11-05 15:44).

**Out of scope:** reviewer account pools (a later leaf may reuse the picker for `review delegate`); storing credentials or tokens in any file; harnesses other than claude-code and codex; failover across roster entries beyond what orchestrate assign already does with an unavailable entry.

## Approach

- `accounts:` lives in M16.5.5's typed `run:` block (version-2 roster), so it is per harness and schema-checked. Only directories are stored; claude-code gets `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR`, and codex keeps its isolated per-profile home (one per model and account) linking that account's auth.json (plan review 2026-11-05).
- Pick rule: lowest (launches + 1) / weight among usable accounts, the dispatcher's rule, so 60/40 weights give a 60/40 split. Weight 0 drains.
- Affinity: a native session lives in one account's directory, so `message` and `resume` must use the recorded account; when it is drained or gone, start a fresh session (same worker id) and say so, as the dispatcher did.
- Failover: a usage-limit failure (the classification M18.10 adds for reviewers) pauses that account (`accounts.<id>.paused_until` on the entry's roster patch, never entry-level `available: false`) in the gitignored overlay `orchestration.local.yaml`, which orchestrate plan already reads; `worker resume` then re-sends once on another account. When all accounts are paused, the entry is unavailable to orchestrate assign, which falls back to the next eligible executor (the dispatcher's "Grok when Opus is limited" rule, generalised).
- Depends on M16.5.5 (`run:` block), M16.5.1 and M16.5.3 (end-state modules) and M18.10 (limit classification and overlay writer).
