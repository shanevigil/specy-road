## Intent

Make the coordinator's dispatcher a product feature: one `grind-session` run keeps several workers busy, schedules leaves so that no two live claims share a zone, caps live workers per model, hands finished leaves to their reviewers and lands each leaf whose gate is met. Today `grind-session` lands one leaf at a time, and the 0.3.1 grind needed `dispatch.py`, `pick.sh`, `hold.txt` and three start scripts to do this by hand.

**Out of scope:** automatic fix rounds (M18.21), the supervision report (M18.22), account pools (M18.23), running the reviewers themselves (M18.24 `review run`), a daemon or service mode (the user's own supervisor restarts the loop), and any LLM call by the toolkit outside the rostered harness launches `worker delegate` already makes.

## Approach

- Build on `grind-session` (moved to `specy_road/orchestration/grind/` by M16.19) and its planner: readiness from RoadmapIndex, claims from the M16.15 snapshot, zone overlap from `shared_zones.py` (the same rule as `--plan`'s parallel batches). `--workers 1` (the default) keeps today's one-leaf loop, so the M16.19 grind table is unchanged.
- One tick = three passes in this order: land (gate met at the branch tip, keyed on the registry claim, not on a worker record: OD 09:15 and RE 2026-11-05 09:20 show a missing record must not strand an approved leaf), hand off to review (READY at tip, clean post-run check, no record at tip), delegate (free slots under `--workers` and `policy.max_workers_per_model`). Re-delegate a claim with no live worker, no READY and no worker record (M15.26 already lists such claims).
- Per-model cap: `policy.max_workers_per_model` in `orchestration.schema.json`, enforced in `worker delegate`'s slot check (`specy_road/orchestration/workers/`), so a manual delegate obeys it too. The dispatcher's `MAX_OPUS` / `MAX_COMPOSER` / `MAX_GROK` are the evidence.
- Hold list: `work/grind-hold.txt` (gitignored), the product form of `hold.txt`. Held claims hold no zones (OD 06:53: held claims kept their zones and the loop idled about two hours).
- `grind.release_zones_in_review` (default false): the maintainer asked for maximum parallelism on the toolkit's grind; a leaf in review then stops blocking others, and the later lander resolves conflicts (the trial merge before review refuses a real conflict).
- Settings from flags and `git-workflow.yaml` only: a restart from a shell with other environment variables used the wrong account and inherited the git lock (RE 2026-11-05 11:25, 15:44).
- The loop never acts while the integration checkout has uncommitted tracked changes (RE 2026-11-05 15:12: a planner's edit blocked landings).
- Buy vs build: this is the job of a durable workflow engine or a multi-CLI runner; `sr/docs/research/harness-landscape.md` keeps building until a trigger fires (no daemon, no new runtime dependency).
- Depends on M16.14, M16.15, M16.16, M16.19, M16.50 (end-state modules and `grind:` settings) and M18.24 (the review run it calls).
