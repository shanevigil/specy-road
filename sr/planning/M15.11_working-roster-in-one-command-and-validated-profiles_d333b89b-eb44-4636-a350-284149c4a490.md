## Intent

A new repo has no roster and building one took three reads of the docs; the starter does not show the `profile:` format; docs and starter use `claude-opus-5.5` while profiles use `claude-opus-5-5`; a malformed `profile:` passes validate and later produces a misleading "not committed on the integration branch" plus a may_process hint that reads as a second problem. `orchestrate plan` always prints "coordinator: human" and always assigns the cheapest capable executor, so one adopter's 34 leaves all went to one model.

## Approach

- New `specy_road/roster_generate.py` and a `models roster` subcommand in `models_cli.py` (203 lines): entries from shipped profiles (`model_profiles.py`), `--write` appends to the YAML.
- New `specy_road/roster_profile_check.py`: resolve each `profile:` locally; validate calls it (one call line in `validate_roadmap_checks.py`, at 399); `orchestration_pinned.py` reports invalid profiles distinctly and `orchestrate_plan.py` suppresses the dependent hint.
- Fix ids in `docs/orchestration*.md`, the design note and the starter; a test scans roster examples against shipped profile ids.
- Assignment moves to new `specy_road/orchestrate_assign.py` (orchestrate_plan.py is at 372) with `--spread` round-robin; coordinator line.
