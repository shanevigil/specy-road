## Intent

Twice in one night the maintainer asked for status and the coordinator found stale claims. `do-next-available-task` claims a leaf and creates its worktree; when the following `worker delegate` fails, the leaf stays claimed with no worker and holds its touch zones, blocking every overlapping leaf, with nothing reporting it. Two causes were seen: `max_workers` counted finished workers and refused with "19 workers active", and a usage-limit window.

## Approach

- Slots: `worker_orphans.HELD_STATES` includes `completed`, and `holding_slots` counts it unless its leaf is over (M11.57). Count only `starting`, `running`, `idle` and `paused_by_limit`; `completed`, `failed` and `terminated` never count. Adjust `slot_breakdown` so the refusal lists only counted workers. `worker_ops_slots._slot_or_existing` keeps its shape.
- New `specy_road/orphaned_claims.py`: `orphaned_claims(repo_root) -> list[dict]` from the registry (`roadmap/registry.yaml` entries), worker records (`list_records`), live sessions (`session_presence.list_sessions`) and the newest valid READY (`review_files.latest_ready`) compared with the branch tip; `report_lines(...)` with age and the two commands.
- `handoff status` (`review_cli.cmd_handoff_status`; the file is at 400) prints the lines without growing the file. `doctor.py` (311) adds a report-only section outside `FIX_ORDER`.
- `worker delegate` failure: the error print in `worker_cli.main` (397 lines) changes one existing line, using a helper in `orphaned_claims.py` that adds the hint when the leaf is claimed and has no live worker.
- `grind_session_events.py` (165): the `hook_failed` line for `phase=implement` adds the claim note.
- `specyrd-orchestrate.md` step 3: one sentence.
- Overlap: M15.28 moves `cmd_delegate` out of `worker_cli.py`; whichever lands second keeps the hint on the delegate error path.
