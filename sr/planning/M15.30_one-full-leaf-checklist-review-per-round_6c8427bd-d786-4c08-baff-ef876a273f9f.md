## Intent

In an adopter's run, reviews found pre-existing defects one layer at a time: each round probed deeply on what the previous round raised and less on the rest, and reviewer prompts naming specific probes made those pass while the next defect surfaced. The same run's metrics show every late-caught defect came from a second reviewer, and that the one review that ran the CI command caught a lint regression six others missed. Under `policy.delta_review` a fix round can be approved from deltas alone, so nobody re-reads the whole leaf.
