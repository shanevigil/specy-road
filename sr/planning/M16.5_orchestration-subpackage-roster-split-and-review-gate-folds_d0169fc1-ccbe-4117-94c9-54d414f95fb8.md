## Intent

Move workers, roster, profiles, guard, presence, grind and records into `specy_road/orchestration/`. Each move is a pure move followed by a consolidation: the worker fold, `check_assignment`, the roster v2 split and its upgrade, guard rule tables, grind settings. Then fold the review-gate, escalation and packet fragments, and finish with the post-fix complexity gate.

## Approach

Leaves run in id order, which is chain order: every leaf's prerequisites have a smaller id, and no edge points to a later group. There are no group-level dependencies; the leaf edges give the order.

Why together: This is the recommendation's orchestration pass plus the review half of its extras pass, and one contiguous chain segment (M16.5.12 waits on M16.5.11). Most M18 dependencies on M16 land here: M18.1-M18.5, M18.18, M18.19, M18.23 and M18.25 depend only on M16.5 leaves, and M18.20, M18.26 and M18.27 partly, so M16.5 is the natural "M18 is unblocked" group. At 14 leaves it is the largest group; splitting it at M16.5.11/M16.5.12 would need a ninth group or merging M16.6 and M16.7, so it is not done.
