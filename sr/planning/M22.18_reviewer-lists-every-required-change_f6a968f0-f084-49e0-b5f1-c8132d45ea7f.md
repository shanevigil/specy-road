## Intent

Cut review rounds. During the rc4 fix batches, Sol and Grok usually reported one or two required changes per block; each new one cost a full fix-and-review cycle (M21.7 needed seven Sol rounds; a single exhaustive sweep then found 17 issues at once). The coordinator's own reviewer addendum now asks for exhaustive reviews; this leaf gives adopters the same instruction in the product's reviewer prompt.

Out of scope: changing verdict rules, the approve-notes rule, or review policy.

## Approach

Add the instruction to `schema_lines()` in review_record_write.py, which every reviewer prompt appends (`build_prompt` is at the 80-line function limit, so it gains no line). Test it per channel.
