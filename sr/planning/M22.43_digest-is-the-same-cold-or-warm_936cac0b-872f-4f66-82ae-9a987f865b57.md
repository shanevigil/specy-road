## Intent

A field report found `digest --check` passing in a warm checkout and failing in a new worktree at the same commit. The cold render dropped one "dependency removed" line. So CI on a fresh clone can fail on a correct file, `upgrade` can silently drop the line, and two agents can keep rewriting it. The cause is not confirmed: the report suspects the cold history walk misses edges removed on a side branch. Our own per-branch digest drift may be related.

## Approach

Reproduce first with a test history, then find which walk is wrong. Make cold and warm agree on the full result, so the digest depends on the repository alone. If the fix needs `digest.py` itself, stop and name it for the coordinator.
