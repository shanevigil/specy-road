## Intent

The shared-stash post-run check blames whichever worker's turn ends first. One worker ran `git stash push -m <tag>` mid-turn and popped it later; other workers whose turns ended while the entry existed were flagged "stash list changed during the turn", and the stashing worker, whose own before and after lists matched, was not. The stash stack is shared by every worktree of the repository, and its entry subject names the branch it was made on.

## Approach

- `worker_turn_check.git_state`: read `git stash list --format=%H %gs` and keep `(hash, subject)` pairs; `git_findings` hands the before and after pairs to the new module instead of comparing tuples.
- New `specy_road/worker_turn_stash.py`: `stash_branch(subject)` for the `On <b>:` and `WIP on <b>:` forms; `stash_findings(repo_root, rec, before, after)` that finds the owner among `worker_state.list_records` by `branch`, appends a `post_run_check` event and `post_run_findings` flag to the owner (`append_event`, `add_flag`), and returns this turn's findings. Record the attributed hashes in the event detail and skip a hash already attributed to that owner.
- M14.4.3 (unclaimed) also lists `worker_turn_check.py`; keep this change to the stash field and comparison.
