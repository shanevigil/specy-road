## Intent

User-level acceptance of the 0.3.1 orchestration view in a real browser on a clean VM, from the wheel adopters install, before the maintainer clears M16.8.9.

Out of scope: CI integration of the sandbox, cross-browser matrices, performance testing, fixing defects (each becomes a new M17 leaf).

## Approach

- The agentic sandbox (VMs reached via MCP tools: claim_workspace, vm_start, sbvm_exec, sbvm_run_browser_test, sbvm_screenshot, ...) is the maintainer's resource: the coordinator checks with the maintainer before using it, every time this leaf is run.
- The seeded state reuses M16.3.1's parity fixtures under tests/parity/orchestration_state/ where they fit, so the GUI is tested on the same states the CLI is.
- Human-led: a type: gate is the only enforcing way to keep a leaf out of the pickup queue; this leaf relies on its dependency on M17.8 and the coordinator's dispatch. Depends on: M17.8.
