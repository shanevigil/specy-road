## Intent

Runbook drift found while running docs/agentic-sandbox-runbook.md against rc4 with real harnesses (pre-release evidence 2026-11-05, D11 and D14). The product behaved correctly each time; the runbook's expected results were wrong.

- §1.1 expects `export --check` and `digest --check` to exit 1 after `init project`. Since M21.9, init writes `roadmap.md` and `roadmap-context.md`, so both exit 0.
- §5 says "Claim two leaves" from one checkout. The second `do-next-available-task` exits 6 unless it is given `--allow-second-claim`, and the runbook does not mention this.
- §2.6 says one edited byte makes the gate report "does not verify". The message depends on where the byte is: a front-matter value gives "signature does not verify for '<model>'", a notes-body byte gives "notes body does not match notes_sha256", and an edit that breaks the schema (for example an `A1: Pass` value) makes the record schema-invalid.
- §4.1 says `models list` shows a `stale` flag. It has no such column. Staleness is shown by `models stale` (exit 1 when a rostered profile was researched more than 180 days ago) and `models show <id>`. Decision: fix the runbook, not the command.

Out of scope: product changes; the runbook's other sections; §6 and §9 (not run in full at rc4).

Overlaps checked (2026-11-05): M20's probe leaf adds a runbook step later (after 0.3.1), and the two do not conflict. No M21 or M22 leaf lists the runbook.

## Approach

Text-only edits at the four places. Each expected result is stated as the rc4 run observed it, with the command and exit code.
