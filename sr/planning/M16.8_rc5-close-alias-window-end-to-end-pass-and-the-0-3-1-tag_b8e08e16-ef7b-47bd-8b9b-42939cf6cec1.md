## Approach

Leaves run in id order, which is chain order: every leaf's prerequisites have a smaller id, and no edge points to a later group. There are no group-level dependencies; the leaf edges give the order.

Why together: The alias-window close, the end-to-end pass, the release docs and the tag gate come last. M16.8.9 is what M18, M19 and M20 depend on. New fix leaves from M16.8.4 go here (`add-node --parent M16.8`, then `move-node --index` to place them before M16.8.8), and the planner adds them to M16.8.9's dependencies. M16.8.5-M16.8.7 are Complete and stay (M16.8.8 and M16.8.9 depend on them); while they do, M16.8 rolls up as Not Started with 3 of 9 leaves Complete.
