## Tier

trust boundary: it adds a condition that refuses landing.

## Done but wrong

- A result still counts after the check changed: editing the entry's `run` or `zones` after `ready` makes land refuse.
- A deletion or rename slips through: a leaf whose only zoned change is a deleted file, or a file renamed out of a zone, runs the entry.
- One landing path skips it: land refuses a missing or failed zoned result under `land: local` as well as `land: push`.

## If this fails

Most likely to break: the commit that lands is the commit `ready` scored. Finish adds bookkeeping commits before landing, so every zoned result could read as stale. If so, the maintainer decides which commits a result covers.

## Accepted limits

- A leaf merged on the forge (`on_complete: pr`) is not blocked by specy-road.
