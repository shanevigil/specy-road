## Policy-to-evaluator table

`python scripts/review_effort_study.py policy-evidence [NODE_ID ...] [-o DIR]` runs no reviewer and no network. A case is one review round: the node_key, the commit and every record filed at that commit. The records are the `history/` copies plus each live `REVIEW-*.md` that no history record duplicates (same model, commit and verdict). Rounds come in `review_rca.review_rounds` order, then the commits that only a live record names (round `live`; the RCA rule reads history alone, so it never counted them). A record's filing time is its history stamp, or, for a record known only by its local `date`, that whole day in any time zone (UTC-12 to UTC+14), starting no earlier than the reviewed commit. A round whose time is such an interval is replayable only if no integration commit inside the interval changed `orchestration.yaml` or `git-workflow.yaml`; otherwise it is cited-only or unknown. It is replayable when every record validates and has a filing time, the commit and the node (from its chunk at that commit, via the manifest for an archived node) come back from git, and `roadmap/orchestration.yaml` and `roadmap/git-workflow.yaml` load at the integration branch's last first-parent commit before the round's latest filing time (the interval's end). Defaults fill the policy keys the file leaves out. `cases.csv` lists each case's provenance. Each policy's evaluator then runs twice: once with the policy as configured, and once with only that policy disabled. It screens out cases with no difference. A change counts only when no other policy's evaluator moves as well. For a gate policy (all except RCA escalation and agreement), the change must also show in the complete gate outcome. `review_gate.evaluate_gate` replays the case in a throwaway shared clone with no remote, checked out at the case's integration commit, so it reads that policy, registry, roadmap and decisions. The clone gets the node's history up to the round, the round's records, and today's READY, RCA, PRE-EXISTING and DEFERRAL files. The gate runs with the configured policy and again with only the selected policy disabled: keys written into a commit of `orchestration.yaml`, or for pre-existing and deferral, their files left out. Every other condition is the same in both runs. The outcome compared is passed, the counted approvals, the counted blocks, the verifier problems and the unmet strong-review items. A replay whose gate cannot read its policy is not replayable. A case that cannot be replayed for a policy is cited-only when a record field or problem sentence (the record's front matter) names the policy. Otherwise it is unknown.

| Policy | Public evaluator | Disabled as | Outcome compared | Counted as changed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| delta | `review_gate.delta_problem`, then `evaluate_gate` | `delta_review: false` | whether each approval naming `delta_from` counts | a delta approval counted that is refused without it |
| resumed delta | `review_resumed_delta.fresh_session_problem`, then `evaluate_gate` | `reviewer_resumed_delta: false` | whether each approval passes the fresh-session rule | a resumed approval counted that is refused without it |
| red-green | `red_green.outcome_problems` via `integrity_gate`, then `evaluate_gate` | `red_green: false` | gate problems | a gate block it caused (with the key on, RED-GREEN.md and planned_test_changes are not reconstructed, so the case is not replayable) |
| pre-existing | `review_pre_existing.gate_problems`, then `evaluate_gate` | no claim honoured (PRE-EXISTING.md left out of the replay) | failing READY verifiers left as gate problems | a failing verifier it cleared (needs READY and PRE-EXISTING.md at the commit) |
| deferral | `review_deferral.deferral_in_effect`, then `evaluate_gate` | no marker applied (DEFERRAL files left out of the replay) | DEFERRAL markers in effect that resolve a block of the round | a deferral it forced |
| RCA escalation | `review_rca.escalation` | both thresholds out of reach | the escalation rule that starts at this round (onset only) | an RCA escalation it forced |
| strong review | `review_strong.applicable_items`, then `evaluate_gate` | `strong_review: []` | applicable items that no approval of the round meets | a gate block it caused |
| agreement | `review_gate_agreement`'s rule | no check | an `orchestration.yaml` asking for reviewers while `require_implementation_review_before_finish` is false or absent (absent is off) | a validate or doctor error it raised (no GateResult field) |

A change in the suggested next step alone is not counted. Baseline: an RCA record filed at a round, other than a forced one, must replay as an escalation under the configured policy, or the case is unknown for RCA. Every RCA record replayed. For the record-level policies the records hold reviewer verdicts, not the gate's verdict, so the configured run is the baseline.

## Evidence (counted 2026-11-08)

Recounted 2026-11-08 after review round 2. Source: the main checkout's `sr/work/reviews`, with history and the live records no history copy duplicates. That gives 872 cases from 310 nodes, filed from 2026-10-28 to 2026-11-08, against 387 distinct integration commits. 828 cases have full provenance. The 44 others are known only by a record's `date`, and the config changed inside their filing interval, so they cannot be reconstructed exactly. All 30 cases that only a live record names are among them. The run used `PATH=/usr/bin:/bin` and `unshare -rn`, so no reviewer CLI and no network.

| Policy | Replayable | Changed | Cited-only | Unknown |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| delta | 828 | 5 | 0 | 44 |
| resumed delta | 828 | 0 | 0 | 44 |
| red-green | 828 | 0 | 0 | 44 |
| pre-existing | 825 | 0 | 5 | 42 |
| deferral | 828 | 0 | 0 | 44 |
| RCA escalation | 828 | 62 | 2 | 42 |
| strong review | 828 | 0 | 0 | 44 |
| agreement | 828 | 0 | 0 | 44 |

Through the complete gate, delta changed 5 rounds: M15.22 round 4, M15.27 round 7, M15.35 round 2, M21.3 round 3 and M22.47 round 2. The delta rule passed in 6 more rounds (M11.19, M11.24, M11.60, M11.65, M8.3, M8.7), but there the gate refused the delta approval or closed for another reason whether or not delta was on.

Read with these limits:

- This project's committed policy never turned on `reviewer_resumed_delta`, `red_green` or `strong_review`. `require_implementation_review_before_finish` was always true. No DEFERRAL marker exists, and no PRE-EXISTING.md was kept. So zero changes there means the policy was unused or unknown here, not that it is useless.
- The replay runs today's evaluator code and today's `DEFAULT_POLICY` defaults on reconstructed config, not the code as it was then.
- `delta_problem` reads today's PACKET.md and claim zones, and deferral reads today's `roadmap/decisions.yaml`.
- The gate replay uses today's READY, RCA, PRE-EXISTING and DEFERRAL files (READY history is not kept). That affects both runs equally, but the replay may still differ from the gate as it ran then.
- Records on other machines, or deleted before the count, are not counted.

No policy is recommended for deprecation on this evidence.
