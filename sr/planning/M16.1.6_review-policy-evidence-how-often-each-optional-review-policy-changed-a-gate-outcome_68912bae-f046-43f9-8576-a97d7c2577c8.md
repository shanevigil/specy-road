## Policy-to-evaluator table

`python scripts/review_effort_study.py policy-evidence [NODE_ID ...] [-o DIR]` runs no reviewer and no network. A case is one review round: the node_key, the commit and every record filed at that commit. The records are the `history/` copies plus each live `REVIEW-*.md` that no history record duplicates (same model, commit and verdict). Rounds come in `review_rca.review_rounds` order, then the commits that only a live record names (round `live`; the RCA rule reads history alone, so it never counted them). A record's filing time is its history stamp, or else the end of its own `date` day (UTC). It is replayable when every record validates and has a filing time, the commit and the node (from its chunk at that commit, via the manifest for an archived node) come back from git, and `roadmap/orchestration.yaml` and `roadmap/git-workflow.yaml` load at the integration branch's last first-parent commit before the round's latest filing time. Defaults fill the policy keys the file leaves out. `cases.csv` lists each case's provenance. Each policy's evaluator then runs twice: once with the policy as configured, and once with only that policy disabled. A change counts only when no other policy's evaluator moves as well. A case that cannot be replayed for a policy is cited-only when a record field or problem sentence (the record's front matter) names the policy. Otherwise it is unknown.

| Policy | Public evaluator | Disabled as | Outcome compared | Counted as changed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| delta | `review_gate.delta_problem` | `delta_review: false` | whether each approval naming `delta_from` counts | a delta approval counted that is refused without it |
| resumed delta | `review_resumed_delta.fresh_session_problem` | `reviewer_resumed_delta: false` | whether each approval passes the fresh-session rule | a resumed approval counted that is refused without it |
| red-green | `red_green.outcome_problems` via `integrity_gate` | `red_green: false` | gate problems | a gate block it caused (with the key on, RED-GREEN.md and planned_test_changes are not reconstructed, so the case is not replayable) |
| pre-existing | `review_pre_existing.gate_problems` | no claim honoured | failing READY verifiers left as gate problems | a failing verifier it cleared (needs READY and PRE-EXISTING.md at the commit) |
| deferral | `review_deferral.deferral_in_effect` (as `apply_deferrals` uses it) | no marker applied | DEFERRAL markers in effect that resolve a block of the round | a deferral it forced |
| RCA escalation | `review_rca.escalation` | both thresholds out of reach | the escalation rule that starts at this round (onset only) | an RCA escalation it forced |
| strong review | `review_strong.applicable_items` | `strong_review: []` | applicable items that no approval of the round meets | a gate block it caused |
| agreement | `review_gate_agreement`'s rule | no check | an `orchestration.yaml` asking for reviewers while `require_implementation_review_before_finish` is false or absent (absent is off) | a validate or doctor error it raised (no GateResult field) |

A change in the suggested next step alone is not counted. Baseline: an RCA record filed at a round, other than a forced one, must replay as an escalation under the configured policy, or the case is unknown for RCA. Every RCA record replayed. For the record-level policies the records hold reviewer verdicts, not the gate's verdict, so the configured run is the baseline.

## Evidence (counted 2026-11-08)

Recounted 2026-11-08 after review round 1. Source: the main checkout's `sr/work/reviews`, with history and the 60 live records no history copy duplicates (28 nodes). That gives 871 cases from 310 nodes, filed from 2026-10-28 to 2026-11-08T09:06Z, against 389 distinct integration commits. All 871 have full provenance. 30 cases are commits only a live record names. In 44 cases at least one record's time comes from its `date` field, so the config found may be up to a day late. The run used `PATH=/usr/bin:/bin` and `unshare -rn`, so no reviewer CLI and no network.

Read with these limits:

- This project's committed policy never turned on `reviewer_resumed_delta`, `red_green` or `strong_review`. `require_implementation_review_before_finish` was always true. No DEFERRAL marker exists, and no PRE-EXISTING.md was kept. So zero changes there means the policy was unused or unknown here, not that it is useless.
- The replay runs today's evaluator code and today's `DEFAULT_POLICY` defaults on reconstructed config, not the code as it was then.
- `delta_problem` reads today's PACKET.md and claim zones, and deferral reads today's `roadmap/decisions.yaml`.
- Strong-review approvals are the latest approval per model and are not checked again for eligibility.
- Records on other machines, or deleted before the count, are not counted.
