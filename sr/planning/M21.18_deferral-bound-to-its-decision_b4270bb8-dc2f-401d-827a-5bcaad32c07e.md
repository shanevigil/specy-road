## Intent

Release blocker F3 plus review W4. A deferral marker is meant to be a cache of one human decision. Today the gate treats it as more than that.

- `apply_deferrals` (review_deferral.py:239-260) removes a counted blocking review when a `DEFERRAL-*.yaml` marker names the reviewer and commit, its items still qualify, and `marker["decision"]` is any closed decision on the node (`_closed_decisions`, :227). The gate does not check that the decision was recorded for this deferral, or that the marker's RCA exists. A worker who can write the gitignored `work/reviews/<ID>/` can hand-write a marker that cites any unrelated closed decision and drop a counted block without the human-only `review defer`. sol verified this in memory: an answer of "Use blue for the button." removed the block. tests/test_review_defer.py:243 (`_forge_closed_deferral`, answer "x") and tests/test_review_deferral_agreement.py:64 (LEDGER D9) lock that behaviour in.
- W4. `review_next_step.facts` (:152) and `rework_rca.governing_rca` (:91) call a deferral "applied" when the marker file merely exists. A malformed marker, one whose decision is open, or one that no longer qualifies still selects the applied step and can become the governing rework design.

Security lens: review decisions stay human-owned. The fix only narrows what the gate accepts. It adds no new way to defer, no `--yes`, no agent-run path.

Out of scope: reading the ledger from the integration ref instead of the working tree (sol's stronger proposal; a structured authorization record would need a decisions-schema change; maintainer to decide for a later release); `review defer`'s CLI flow and prompts; the qualification rule itself (`qualify_deferral`); review_tally (rc5 M22.11); symlink handling of `write_marker`.

Overlaps checked (2026-11-05): M21.21 edits review_delegate_harness.py and review_model_check.py, not these files. M21.23 edits docs/orchestration-review.md:322 (delegate procedure). This leaf edits its :241 deferral paragraph only.

## Approach

- One predicate in review_deferral.py, `deferral_in_effect(repo_root, node_id, marker) -> list[str]` (problems; empty means in effect). It is the only place the gate, the next step and the rework design decide "applied". In effect means all of these hold:
  1. The marker is shape-valid (`load_markers`).
  2. The RCA it names exists under the node's reviews dir, is valid, has `recommended_action.type == defer_findings`, and its `trigger.round` equals the marker's `round`.
  3. The marker's `deferred` items equal that RCA's `deferred_findings` on reviewer, commit, finding, category, acceptance_id and input_class.
  4. The decision it names is closed, on this node, of kind `decision`, and was recorded for this deferral as `review defer` records it (review_defer.py `_record`): `why_now` starts with `<rca file> recommends defer_findings`, and `answer` starts with `Deferred by ` and contains `from <rca file>:` and each deferred finding's text.
  5. `qualify_deferral` accepts the items now (unchanged).
- `apply_deferrals` uses the predicate in place of `m.data["decision"] in closed`. The block's ineligible reason stays `deferred by <id>`.
- `facts` sets `DEFER_APPLIED` only when the covering RCA's marker is in effect. Otherwise it stays `DEFER_UNAPPLIED`, and a marker that exists but is not in effect gets its problem lines into the refusal text. `governing_rca` accepts a `defer_findings` RCA only when its marker is in effect.
- Tests: the two forging helpers write a ledger entry the way `review defer` writes it (so the agreement table still means "same outcome for defer and gate"). New negative cases, each leaving the block in `GateResult.blocks` and `facts` not `DEFER_APPLIED`:
  - a hand-written marker citing an unrelated closed decision on the node (the old D9 "x");
  - a closed decision on another node;
  - an open decision;
  - a missing RCA file;
  - an RCA whose round or deferred_findings differ from the marker;
  - a marker altered after a genuine `review defer` (one item's finding changed).
  The genuine `review defer` path still removes the block.
- docs/orchestration-review.md:241 and docs/design-notes/review-loop-rca.md (~:207-213) state the binding.
