## Intent

Integrity findings only inform reviewers until the gate acts on them. Let a project turn them into gate problems, with waivers an executor cannot plant from its own branch. Design: `docs/design-notes/regression-prevention.md`.

## Approach

New `specy_road/integrity_gate.py` reads `policy.integrity_gate` and the node's `integrity_waivers` from the integration branch through the same committed-copy reads the policy uses (`review_policy_ref`, `roadmap_load_at_ref`), runs `integrity` for the commit under review and returns problems; `review_gate.evaluate_gate` appends them (keep `review_gate.py` under the cap). Update both template schemas and run `specy-road refresh-schemas` for `sr/schemas/`. Check `suggested_prompts/` for node-field lists. Verify with the gate tests and `specy-road validate`.
