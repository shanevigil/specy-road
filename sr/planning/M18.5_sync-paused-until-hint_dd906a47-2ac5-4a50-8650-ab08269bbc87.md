## Intent

When a worker stops at a usage limit, M8.2 records it as `paused_by_limit` with `limit_reset_at`. The roster's `paused_until` (M11.39) is how a coordinator keeps routing away from that model until the limit resets. Today nothing connects the two: the coordinator has to copy the reset time by hand. M11.39's A3 asked `worker sync` to print the line, but it was dropped because M8.2 had not landed. M8.2 has since landed, and no worker code prints the hint.
