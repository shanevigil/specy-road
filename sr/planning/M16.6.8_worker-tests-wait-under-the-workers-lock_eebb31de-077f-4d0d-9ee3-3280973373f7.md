## Intent

`tests/test_worker_max_workers_global.py` fails in about one run in five, on WIP and on any branch. It has failed landing gates four times (M16.3.1, M16.3.7, M16.6.3, and M16.6.7, which it parked).

The cause is a test-side race. At the end of a turn, the runner clears the record's pid and then releases the lease, both under `workers_lock`. `wait_for_worker` reads the record without that lock, so it can return in the gap, and the test's next `remote_leases(...) == []` sees the lease still there. Resume takes the lock, so the product path is safe.

Out of scope: any product change and any change to the lease logic.

## Approach

- In `tests/support/harness.py`, read the record inside `with workers_lock(repo):` (public, `specy_road/orchestration/workers/worker_lock.py`) on each poll. The pid is then cleared only together with the lease release.
- Change `test_worker_max_workers_global.py` only if a test there waits some other way.
- Show the flake before the change (loop at the merge base) and its absence after (20 of 20 at the head).
