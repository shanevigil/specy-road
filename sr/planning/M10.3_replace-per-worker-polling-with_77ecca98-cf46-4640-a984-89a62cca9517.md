## Intent

Coordination latency is the largest-variance row in the leaf timeline: an event written by a worker waits until the coordinator's next turn. The seed article observes that agents polling with sleep loops pick 10 to 20 second minimums. A foreground wait inside one `sync` call returns the moment something happens and is not a daemon: it lives exactly as long as the tool call (constitution principle 5; design decision D3).

## Approach

In `specy_road/worker_state.py` `append_event` touch `work/.workers/FLAG` after the write. In `specy_road/worker_ops.py` add `wait_for_event(repo_root, timeout, since)` with a 250 ms stat loop; `worker_cli.py` (cap 400) wires `--wait` and `--timeout` onto `sync` and maps timeout to exit 2. Document in `docs/orchestration-workers.md` and `docs/grind-session.md`. Verify with `pytest tests/test_worker_cli.py tests/test_worker_state.py`.

Converge verdict (brainstorm `agentic-performance`): States C3 best (B3 and B33 are the same bet). Principle 5 holds: foreground only. Docs: orchestration-workers.md loop and command table; grind-session.md step mode.
