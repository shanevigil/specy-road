## Intent

Score reviewers from what they actually did, not only from the roster. A consumer run and this toolkit's own run both showed one reviewer approving nearly everything on the first round while another blocked every leaf at least once with findings that held up. Per-model numbers from recorded reviews let a coordinator see that, and, opt-in, let `orchestrate plan` weigh it.

## Approach

- New `specy_road/review_model_stats.py`: over the last N review records across leaves (default 50; local `work/reviews/*/REVIEW-*.md` via `review_files.list_reviews`, plus M12.4.1's tracked `Review-tally` trailers for leaves whose local records are gone), per model: reviews, block rate, *overturned approvals* (an approve at commit C where another model blocked C and the leaf's next reviewed commit differs from C), mean `probes` per approve (M14.5, when present) and mean `commands_rerun` entries. Deterministic ordering; no wall-clock input.
- `specy-road models stats` in `specy_road/bundled_scripts/models_cli.py` prints the table; `--json`.
- `policy.evidence_scoring` (default false) and `policy.evidence_overturn_threshold`: in `specy_road/orchestrate_plan.py`, a reviewer above the threshold has its reviewer score lowered by a fixed step, and the plan line says why. Off → plans unchanged.
- `docs/orchestration-models.md` documents the stats and the policy.
- **Verify.** Tests with fixture records: block rate and overturned count; plan with policy on/off.
