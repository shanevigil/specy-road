## Intent

A project's human may ratify scope only through some phase, for example the first few phases of a baseline. In one adopter repo the roadmap then grew well past that boundary (production hosting, real-data cutover, releases), and nothing recorded or flagged that the later phases had never been ratified. This leaf stores the boundary as a human decision in the ledger and has validate and the digest name the phases beyond it.

**Out of scope:**
- Blocking pickup, add-node or any other command on nodes beyond the boundary. These are notices only.
- More than one boundary per project.
- Inferring a boundary from prose.

## Approach

- Source: adopter feedback on 0.3.1rc3 (item 28, sanitized), filed 2026-11-05.
- Storage: a new ledger kind, `scope`, rather than a manifest field. Scope ratification is a human decision with an author, a date and a history (`--supersedes`), which the ledger already models. The entry reuses `node` for the last ratified phase or milestone.
- "Beyond" means the later siblings of the boundary node and of each of its ancestors, in outline order. Each is listed once, without its descendants. A boundary at a milestone therefore also lists the later phases.
- The digest line goes in the open-decisions section that M19.14 adds, so the leaf depends on M19.14. The agent rule M19.14 adds gains one sentence about recording a ratification as `--kind scope`.
- Schema: the `kind` enum gains `scope`, and a conditional requires `node` and forbids the fields of the other kinds. The change goes in the bundled schema and this repo's `sr/schemas/` copy, and refresh-schemas or upgrade delivers it.
- Depends on M19.14 (maintainer rule), M16.49 (decisions code location), M16.13 (`decisions_cli.py` shape) and M16.44 (`validate_roadmap.py` after the fold).
