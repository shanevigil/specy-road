## Intent

Found in rc2 field use (2026-11-02). Sending a follow-up while a worker's turn was running was refused with 'cannot steer a live turn', so the coordinator had to poll and resend.

## Approach

Store the queued text on the worker record under the workers lock; when the wrapper finishes a turn and finds a queued message, resume with it as the next turn. Verify with the touched test files and file-limits.
