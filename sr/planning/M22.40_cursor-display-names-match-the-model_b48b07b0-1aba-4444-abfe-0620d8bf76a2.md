## Intent

Cursor reports names like `Claude Opus 5.5 300K High`. `normalise` keeps the context size, so `same_model` fails against `claude-opus-5-5-high`, and an honest report files a full paid review as model_mismatch. Confirmed for Opus, Sol and Grok names. Adopters cannot get a counted Cursor review that reports its model.

## Approach

Strip the context-size token and map the effort word in the comparison. Keep the check strict: a different model or effort must still mismatch. This does not change how the gate treats an unreported model, which is decided after 0.3.1.
