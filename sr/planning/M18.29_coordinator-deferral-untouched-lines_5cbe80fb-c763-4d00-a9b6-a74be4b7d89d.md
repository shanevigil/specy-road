## Intent

`review defer` is human-only. The retrospective found that late rounds mostly surface true findings one input class at a time. This leaf lets the coordinator end such a loop at the fix-round budget by moving the remaining findings into ONE follow-up leaf with a bounded acceptance ("one bounded rule, limits documented"), under a standing rule a person recorded for exactly this action and scope. It is the product side of the restricted R3 in `sr/work/planner-2026-11-11-review-rules/proposal-v2.md`, phase 2 only.

Every restriction narrows the existing human form. The coordinator form never applies on a trust-boundary leaf, never to regression, test-integrity, security, data-loss or gate-integrity findings, never before the budget is spent, never on a changed line, and never when the project has set no fix-risk paths. It keeps every protection of the human form, including the earlier-passed-acceptance refusal.

**Out of scope:** choosing which findings to defer (the analyst's `defer_findings` RCA record still does that); round-limited or mid-loop deferral; making follow-ups block a release (M18.30, a dependency, must land first); recording standing rules for any other action.

## Approach

- Extend `review_defer.py`, `qualify_deferral` in `review_deferral.py` and the RCA item schema (`risk`, `locations`); no new command.
- Bind the rule to its action and scope: `decisions add --kind standing-rule --action review-defer --scope <node>` through the person-only path M22.69 adds. A standing rule without an action keeps today's meaning, and authorises nothing here.
- Check role, then rule, then the leaf (budget, Tier, policy), then each item, before writing anything.
- Hunks come from `git diff -U3` between the previous reviewed commit and the blocked commit; locations come from the RCA item's `locations` field, not from parsing finding text.
- The follow-up's first acceptance line is fixed text, so the follow-up never inherits an open-ended "closed for the class" acceptance.

## Tier

trust boundary: the leaf changes which blocks the gate counts and who may record that.
