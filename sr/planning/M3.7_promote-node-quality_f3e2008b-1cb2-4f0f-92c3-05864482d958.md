## Intent

Promoted nodes have a goal, a sane codename and a stable order, and promote reports once what is still missing.

## Approach

promote_session loops add-node style writes; defer validate_at to the end. Codename derivation lives in roadmap_edit_fields (title_to_codename): add a word cap used by promote only, with the existing collision suffix. Accept order is the decision date order in the session.
