## Intent

decisions.yaml entries name their node by display id <!-- historical -->(`node: M16.69`)<!-- /historical -->. A move leaves them naming the old id, which then names nothing or another node, and validate never checks them <!-- historical -->(REHEARSAL.md B3: D8 still said M16.69 after the move to M16.8.7)<!-- /historical -->. After this leaf a move rewrites the ledger's node fields in the same transaction as the registry claims, and validate notices an open entry whose node is gone.

Out of scope: the untracked decisions.local.yaml, switching the ledger to node_key (a schema change; rewriting keeps the file readable and the shape stable), free text in summary, why_now or answer, closed entries in the validate check, and M19.14, M19.15 and M19.18 (ledger features, not this defect).

## Approach

3. Verify with the tests, then on a scratch worktree: move M16.8.7 and check D8.
