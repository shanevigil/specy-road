## Intent

Today specy-road reviews code after implementation. Nothing reviews a leaf's plan before an executor starts: the only spec review is the optional single-pass `review-node` (and the PM GUI's LLM Review), which is advisory and runs in the planner's own context. This leaf adds a **plan review in two stages**, built on `review delegate`:

- **(a) Full plan review right after planning.** Whenever a planner adds leaves or edits acceptance, zones or dependencies (`add-node`, `edit-node`, a batch commit), fresh reviewers from distinct families critique the new or changed leaves on a fixed checklist. Findings are recorded and the planner answers them. Policy can require a current, answered plan review before the leaf is pickable.
- **(b) A light check just before pickup.** Deterministic: has the leaf's acceptance, zones or dependencies changed since its plan review; did a dependency land changes to files in its zones; do its zones still match tracked files; are findings unanswered. Pickup warns, never refuses.

## Approach

- Evidence (coordinator, `sr/work/coordinator-rough-edges.md`):
  - Under-specified acceptance produced review loops in which each round found a new input class (M9.7 encodings, M8.1 YAML layouts).
  - A design term that contradicted an acceptance line (M11.4: `--offline` against "a global limit") looped reviewers for many rounds, each asking for the line to be amended. The same pattern — vague or self-contradictory acceptance driving long review loops — recurred on rc4 leaves.
  - Touch zones missed files that acceptance named, so executors stopped for zones on most M10/M11 leaves (M10.4, M10.5, M10.9, M10.13, M11.4, M11.8, M11.13), a coordinator round trip each.
- **Stage (a) is already run by hand.** From 2026-11-05 the coordinator runs a fresh distinct-family plan review on its own newly planned leaves; this leaf makes that a supported mode with a record and a policy.
- Source of the pattern: `sr/docs/research/elephant-goldfish-2026-11-05.md` (section 4, item 1). Borrow two things from EGM: the "paraphrase first" comprehension test, and resolving each finding explicitly. Do not depend on EGM or vendor its prompts.
- Reuse, no new entrypoint: a plan mode of `review delegate` (same roster pick, distinct families, fresh bare sessions, same filing path), a record under `work/reviews/<ID>/`, and the existing answer flow (`review_answer.py`) for the planner's replies.
- Stage (a) fixed checklist, one row each:
  - per acceptance line: the reviewer's paraphrase, and whether it can be restated as a checkable outcome;
  - contradictions between acceptance lines (or between acceptance and the sheet);
  - files or paths that acceptance names but the touch zones miss;
  - open-ended detection rules ("flags any …", "handles all …") with no stated bound;
  - scope bounded (what is out of scope is stated);
  - dependencies right (nothing missing, nothing circular or stale).
- The record stores a fingerprint of the acceptance, zones and dependencies the reviewers read; stage (b) compares against it. Stage (b) uses no model.
- Related items, not duplicated here:
  - `review-node` (single-pass advisory LLM review; stays as is);
  - M18.12 and M18.13 (deterministic checks for role-observed acceptance and zones at plan time; this leaf is the reader test beside them);
  - batch H item H1.4, "stale brief after pickup" (acceptance edited after pickup; backlog in the M16.34 sheet). Stage (b) covers edits up to pickup; H1.4 covers edits after it.
- Depends on M16.21 (review fold) and M16.17 (roster and role prompts move into `specy_road/orchestration/`). The zones name today's files plus that end state; re-check them before pickup. The phase starts after the 0.3.1 tag gate.
