## Intent

Two kinds of drift go unnoticed between a node and its planning sheet:

- **Acceptance drift.** Many projects copy a leaf's acceptance into a `## Acceptance criteria` section of its sheet. After `edit-node --add-acceptance` or `--remove-acceptance` the two diverge and validate stays silent. In one adopter repo, reviewers caught this on three nodes, and one node briefly had a contradicting pair of criteria, one line in the sheet and one in the node.
- **Gate dependencies.** `add-dependency` on a `type: gate` node changes neither the gate's acceptance nor its sheet. A human could then clear the gate against a checklist that is missing a prerequisite.

This leaf adds two validate notices and an add-dependency reminder.

**Out of scope:**
- Rewriting any sheet. There is no `edit-node --sync-sheet` (see Approach).
- Requiring an `## Acceptance criteria` section.
- Comparing sheet prose other than that section and, for gates, the whole sheet text.
- Failing validate.

## Approach

- Source: adopter feedback on 0.3.1rc3 (items 25 and 26, sanitized, merged into one leaf because both compare a node with its sheet), filed 2026-11-05.
- Normalised comparison: strip list markers (`-`, `*`, `1.`), checkboxes, backticks and trailing punctuation, collapse whitespace and lowercase. Compare the two as multisets, and report lines found only in the node and lines found only in the sheet.
- "Named" for a gate dependency means the dependency's display id or codename appears as a whole word in the gate's acceptance or anywhere in its sheet. The notice points at the gate sheet template's `## Criteria to clear` section.
- **Decision: no `edit-node --sync-sheet`.** It is not cheap. It would need to rewrite a human-edited Markdown section, choose a canonical line format, and settle which side wins when both changed. The notice already prints the exact lines to copy, so the sync gains little over a hand edit.
- Code goes in a new `validate_sheet_drift.py`, with one call line in `validate_roadmap.py`. `roadmap_crud_dependency_ops.py` is at 398 of 400 lines, so the add-dependency reminder is a helper in the new module and that file gains one call line.
- Depends on M16.44, which folds `validate_notices.py` into `validate_roadmap.py`; touch zones are the post-fold paths. M21.5 and M21.6 also touch `roadmap_crud_dependency_ops.py`. They are in the current rc line and land first.
