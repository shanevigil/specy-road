## Intent

rc4 fix routed through M16.8.4's intake (maintainer rule 2026-11-05: no new leaves in old milestones). `move-node` renumbers display ids across the whole outline, so moving one leaf renames siblings' and other milestones' ids, their planning sheets and claims, and breaks every id cited in sheets, acceptance and coordination notes. Found while restructuring the roadmap into themed milestones (M18, M19); those moves wait on this fix.

## Approach

- In `roadmap_move_node.move_node`, replace the whole-outline `renumber_display_ids_inplace` call with an id assignment for the moved subtree only (next free child id under the new parent; descendants renumbered relative to it).
- Keep the old behaviour behind `--renumber-outline`; the GUI's `move_node_outline` path is unchanged.
- Reuse the existing atomic plan for sheets, registry ids and touch-zone text, limited to the renamed set.
