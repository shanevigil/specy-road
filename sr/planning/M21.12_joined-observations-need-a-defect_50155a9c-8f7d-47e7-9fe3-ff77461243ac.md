## Intent

Stop refusing review records for prose style. The joined-observation rule (M15.17, relaxed for commas and "and" by M15.37) still refuses a covered clause when two neutral parts are joined by "plus", "but", "while" and similar words, even when neither part names a defect. On 2026-11-05 it refused both of Grok's attempts on M21.8, because Grok quoted the leaf's own acceptance wording ("two completions plus a non-running record"); each refusal costs a reviewer turn and leaves the leaf idle. The rule exists to stop a reviewer from hiding a second defect behind one explanation, so it only needs to fire when a joined part names a defect. Fix the class, not the word list.

Out of scope: the defect word list, the explanation markers, and the other clause rules.

## Approach

Add one condition to `_second_observation`: return `None` unless some found part names a defect. Update the docstring. Convert the refused M21.8 lines into table rows; re-baseline rows that only covered neutral joins and list them below.
